
Circles at a Glance
Circles is a protocol reimagining the creation of and access to money.
Imagine a financial system where everyone can exchange value freely through trusted connections, without needing banks or central institutions.

In Circles, you build trust networks—like accepting a cheque from a friend but not a stranger. Over time, these trust relationships weave an expanding web of value that can be exchanged across communities and beyond.

What is Circles?
Circles is a decentralized digital currency protocol that gives every participant the ability to create money directly.

Unlike traditional currencies:

There’s no central authority controlling or gatekeeping access.

Each user creates their own CRC continuously.

Trust relationships make these unique tokens interchangeable.

Why Was Circles Created?
Money shapes how value and opportunity flow through society. Traditionally, the power to issue money has been concentrated in central institutions, creating economic imbalances and limiting participation.

With modern cryptography and decentralized technologies, Circles enables a new kind of money that:

Distributes economic power more fairly

Gives equal access to currency creation

Circles addresses today’s monetary system challenges by creating a system where:

Participation is open to everyone

Currency issuance is distributed across all users

Wealth can be built fairly by every participant

Communities can self-organize to meet their economic needs

Circles is an alternative—not an imposition. It invites voluntary adoption through fairness, resilience, and community-driven design.

Why is it Called "Circles"?
The name reflects:

The circular flow of currency in the economy

The social circles of trust that form the foundation of the system

Each person creates their own CRC, connecting with others to form an overlapping network of trust and exchange.

How Does Circles Differ from Traditional Currencies and Cryptocurrencies Like Bitcoin?
Free Access: Circles is free to join. No need to buy in or mine tokens.

Equal Distribution:

Fiat: issued by governments/banks

Bitcoin: mined by a few

Circles: issued equally by everyone (1 CRC per hour)

Trust Through Community: Your currency gains value through trusted relationships.

Designed for Use: Aimed at everyday exchange with a stable, predictable supply.

Why Create a New Kind of Currency Today?
Currency has always evolved—British Pound, Guilder, Spanish Dollar, etc.

Today, with decentralized tech, we can design a system that:

Is more inclusive and adaptable

Builds on social trust networks

Allows group currencies for local use

Gives equal access to all

Circles complements existing systems while supporting a decentralized, collaborative future.

How Does Circles Work?
Circles operates on four core principles:

Universal Access & Continuous Issuance

Anyone can join; each person creates 1 CRC per hour

Built-in Fairness with Daily Burn

CRC balances are reduced by 7% annually (~0.0199% daily)

Peer-Based Trust

Users accept CRC from people they trust

CRC can flow indirectly through chains of trust

Group-Based Trust

Users can convert CRC into group currencies for local trade

How is New CRC Created?
1 CRC/hour is generated by each user

Must be collected daily, with retroactive collection available for up to 14 days

Daily Burn reduces all balances by ~0.02% daily (7% annually)

Why is the Issuance Rate Set at 1 CRC Per Hour?
Two main reasons:

Simplicity & Predictability: Easy to understand and manage

Fairness Over a Lifetime: ~120,000 CRC over 80 years reflects lifetime participation

This time-linked issuance ensures equal opportunity for all users.

How Does Circles Ensure Long-Term Stability?
Daily Burn (negative interest rate) reduces CRC balances by 7% annually. It:

Maintains the value of 1 CRC/hour creation

Ensures late joiners aren’t disadvantaged

Prevents systemic wealth concentration

Over time, balances reach an equilibrium where daily burn matches new CRC creation.

Alternative Explanation
Daily burn reduces the nominal value of money over time—CRC is not transferred like a tax; it’s simply destroyed. This:

Encourages circulation (not hoarding)

Ensures new users can build up CRC quickly

Prevents early adopters from controlling supply

Stabilizes prices in a Circles economy

Are CRC Tradable?
Yes. You can swap CRC on Metri (currently against USDC, EURe, GBPe).

However:

Not all CRC are immediately tradable.

Your tradable amount depends on:

Trust connections

Your affiliate group

The type of CRC you hold

Metri shows your tradable CRC visually.


What Does "Backing" My Circles Account Mean?
Backing links your personal CRC to a reserve of stable assets like USDC. It:

Connects you to the Circles Reserve

Unlocks features like swapping CRC for stablecoins

Shows your support for the values of the protocol

To become a Direct Circles Backer, you must:

Hold 48 CRC

Contribute $100 dollars in stable assets - choose from wBTC, wETH, GNO, sDAI

Be trusted by 3 existing users

Metri highlights Backers with a "Circles Backer" badge.

Indirect Backers
You can also become an Indirect Backer if 3 Backers trust you, even if you’re not a Backer yourself.
